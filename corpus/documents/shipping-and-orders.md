# Shipping and order support

## Delivery times and options
Standard delivery normally takes three to five business days across all domestic regions.
Orders placed before 2:00 PM CET on business days are dispatched the same day via DHL or UPS.
An express shipping option is also available at checkout, offering guaranteed delivery within one to two business days.
Deliveries take place Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

## Order tracking
Tracking becomes available after the order is shipped. Once your package leaves our fulfillment center,
an automated shipping confirmation email is sent containing your tracking number and carrier link.
Tracking information typically updates within 12 to 24 hours of dispatch.

## Order status inquiries
Customers can request the status of their own order using its order number.
Support agents will provide real-time updates regarding whether the order is pending, processing, shipped, or delivered.
For security and privacy, support must not reveal an order belonging to another customer under any circumstances.

## Delivery issues and delays
If a shipment has not arrived within five business days of dispatch, please contact support with your order number.
Our logistics team will initiate an investigation with the carrier to resolve transit delays or arrange a replacement shipment.
