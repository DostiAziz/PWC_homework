# Cancellation policy

## Eligible orders and timeframes
An order can be cancelled at any time before it has shipped, while it is still processing.
Because our fulfillment warehouse operates rapidly, cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible after placing an order.
The customer must confirm the cancellation before the order is changed to ensure the request is intentional.
Once confirmed, the cancellation is executed immediately and an email confirmation is sent.

## Ineligible orders
Shipped, delivered, and previously cancelled orders cannot be cancelled through the chatbot.
Once an order has been packaged and handed over to the courier (shipped status), transit cannot be interrupted.
In such cases, customers can wait for delivery and initiate a standard return within our 30-day return policy.

## Refund processing
When an order is successfully cancelled before shipment, a full refund of the purchase amount (including standard delivery fees) is triggered automatically.
Refunds are credited back to the original payment method within three to five business days depending on the customer's financial institution.
