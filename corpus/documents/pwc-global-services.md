# PwC global services

PwC describes services including audit and assurance, consulting, tax, legal, deals, risk, and technology related support. The available offering depends on the client's needs, location, and the relevant PwC member firm.

## General enquiry handling

Public service descriptions can help a prospective client identify relevant capabilities. They do not establish an engagement, a price, a professional conclusion, a contractual commitment, or a response-time guarantee. A local PwC contact should confirm scope.
