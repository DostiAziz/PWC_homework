# Returns and refunds

## Return eligibility and timeframes
We offer a 30-day return policy from the date of delivery for all products purchased through our store.
Products must be in their original condition, unworn (or unused), and include all original packaging, tags, and accessories.
Items that have been altered, damaged through misuse, or show signs of extended wear are not eligible for return.

## How to initiate a return
To start a return, contact our support team with:
1. Your order number (e.g., ORD-1001).
2. The product you wish to return and the reason for the return.
3. Whether you prefer a refund or an exchange.

Once approved, we will provide a prepaid return shipping label via email.
Pack the item securely in its original packaging and drop it off at your nearest courier location.

## Refund processing
Refunds are processed within three to five business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.
The refund is credited back to the original payment method used at checkout.
Shipping costs for the original order are refunded only if the return is due to a defective product or an error on our part.

## Exchanges
If you prefer an exchange, we will ship the replacement item once the returned product has been received and inspected.
Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the requested item is out of stock, a full refund will be issued instead.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, personalised or custom-made products, and clearance items marked as final sale are not eligible for return or exchange.
