# Warranty and support

## Warranty period and coverage
Products in this prototype include a two-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
The warranty period begins on the verified date of delivery.
Coverage includes component failures, stitching or zipper malfunctions, and electronic defects under normal intended usage.
Accidental damage, unauthorized modifications, water damage beyond rated specifications, and normal wear and tear are excluded.

## Filing a warranty claim
Customers should provide the product and order number when asking for warranty support.
To initiate a claim, contact our support desk with:
1. Your original order number (e.g., ORD-1001) and product identification.
2. A clear photograph or video demonstrating the defect.
3. A brief description of the issue and when it first occurred.

## Repair and replacement process
Once received, our technical team evaluates claims within two to three business days.
If approved, we provide a prepaid return label and either repair the item or ship a brand-new replacement within seven to ten business days.
If the exact model is discontinued, an equivalent or upgraded product will be offered.
